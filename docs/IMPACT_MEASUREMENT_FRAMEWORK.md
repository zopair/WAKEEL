# Wakeel Export — Impact Measurement Framework
## إطار قياس الأثر الاقتصادي والاجتماعي والثقافي والبيئي

**Status:** Proposed framework for a future pilot. No outcomes are reported as achieved.

## 1. Principles
Establish a baseline before launch; separate inputs/outputs/outcomes; separate registration, verification, active participation, completed transaction and paid income; attribute only verified additional outcomes; prevent double counting; disaggregate only lawfully; measure adverse effects; record data quality and uncertainty.

## 3. Attribution and additionality
Distinguish gross transaction value, Wakeel-attributed value, incremental value (which would not reasonably have occurred without Wakeel), and net economic benefit (incremental domestic value added and relevant externalities less incremental public/private costs).

Lead attribution alone does not prove additionality. Use baseline and, where feasible, comparison group of similar suppliers/products not yet onboarded. Adjust for national export growth, exchange rates, commodity prices, seasonality, existing buyer relationships and other programmes. Independent evaluation should review the protocol.

## 4. Reporting cadence
- Weekly: funnel, RFQ status, bottlenecks and disputes.
- Monthly: active users, supplier/buyer pipeline, conversion, payments, complaints and compliance.
- Quarterly: inclusion, skills, artisan benefit, environmental metrics and risk.
- End of pilot: independent evaluation, incremental exports, unit economics and scale/stop recommendation.

## 5. Data governance
Assign an owner to every KPI. Define purpose, lawful basis, access, retention, correction, audit and sharing rules. Avoid sensitive demographic data unless necessary and lawful. Publish aggregates only where re-identification risk is controlled. Donor reporting does not override Egyptian law.

## 6. National context — not Wakeel baselines
- 2025 non-oil exports: USD 48.567bn.
- Financial inclusion: 79% end-June 2026 (CBE).
- 2025 unemployment: 6.3%; youth unemployment ages 15–29: 13.2% (CAPMAS-reported via SIS).

Collect supplier-, buyer-, participant- and shipment-level baselines before pilot.
