# Wakeel Export — International Funding Opportunities Register
## سجل المنح والتمويلات الدولية والفرص المتاحة

**Verification cut-off:** 19 November 2026. Programme ceilings are not Wakeel funding. An opportunity is not secured until an eligible applicant is selected, award conditions are met, an agreement is effective and funds are disbursed.

## 1. Executive view
- **Commercial platform scale-up:** develoPPP Ventures Egypt, if proof of concept, first revenue, annual accounts and matching cash finance exist.
- **Nature-based / biodiversity:** IKI Small Grants, only for eligible local applicant and genuinely non-profit project.
- **Green skills / climate / coastal:** IKI Medium Grants, with eligible Germany-based lead and Egyptian implementing partner(s).
- **Applied industrial R&D:** STDF–CDTI Egypt-Spain Call 11, with Egyptian research institution and Egyptian/Spanish industrial partners.
- **Circular bio-based pilot:** BIOECONOMY GIANTS Open Call II, with eligible consortium, primary producer and TRL 6–8.
- **Research / digital innovation:** Horizon Europe under Egypt's associated-country status, subject to each call.

## 2. Current opportunities

### develoPPP Ventures — Egypt
**Status:** Egypt window published 15 November–31 December 2026. **Support:** €100,000 non-dilutive; at least €100,000 matching cash required. For innovative, privately owned, for-profit Egyptian start-ups with proof of concept, first operating revenue, viable business/financial plan and at least one annual financial statement. Funds support local scaling; start-up formation is not eligible.  
**Wakeel fit:** potentially relevant only after real pilot, first revenue and matching capital. Repository prototype alone is insufficient.  
Official: [Current Egypt application window](https://www.developpp.de/en/application/ventures/) · [Programme requirements](https://www.developpp.de/en/funding-programme/startups/) · [Conditions PDF](https://www.developpp.de/fileadmin/04_Jetzt_bewerben/04.2_IV_Ventures/develoPPP_Ventures_-_Conditions_of_participation.pdf).

### IKI Small Grants — 8th Call 2026
**Status:** open 15 November 2026–8 January 2027. **Grant:** €60,000–€200,000. **Duration:** normally 1–2 years; start Q2 2028 or later. **Theme:** Nature-based Solutions, biodiversity and climate mitigation/adaptation. Local organisations in ODA-eligible countries; no individuals, government organisations or consortia. Applicant generally must be non-profit or private company pursuing a non-profit project, operating at least 3 years, with average annual revenue €60,000–€500,000 over prior 3 years, dedicated accounting and software-based accounts. Multi-country projects are ineligible.  
**Wakeel fit:** not general platform finance; only a separate genuine non-profit nature-based work package with eligible applicant.  
Official: [IKI Small Grants 2026](https://www.international-climate-initiative.com/en/find-funding/small-grants/small-grants-2026/) · [Eligibility details](https://iki-small-grants.de/funding/).

### IKI Medium Grants — 2026
**Status:** open; official deadline 12 January 2027, 11:59 CET. **Grant:** €300,000–€800,000; 2–3 years; total programme budget up to €8m across selected projects. Themes: green skills for local climate action; future-proof coasts and marine ecosystem solutions. Lead must be eligible Germany-based organisation (including qualifying non-profits or companies with permanent establishment/branch/facility) with one or two local implementing organisations.  
**Wakeel fit:** possible only with genuine German lead and Egyptian climate/green-skills/coastal package; generic export marketing is insufficient.  
Official: [IKI Medium Grants 2026](https://www.international-climate-initiative.com/en/find-funding/medium-grants/medium-grants-2026/) · [IKI funding overview](https://www.international-climate-initiative.com/en/find-funding/).

### IKI Large Grants — 2026
**Status:** listed open; deadline 9 February 2027. **Grant:** €5m–€15m per selected project; up to 8 years; multi-organisation/company consortium.  
**Wakeel fit:** low priority, not a start-up grant; consider only in experienced consortium for major climate/biodiversity programme.  
Official: [IKI funding overview](https://www.international-climate-initiative.com/en/find-funding/).

### STDF–CDTI Egypt-Spain Technological Cooperation — Call 11
**Status:** open; Phase I deadline 3 December 2026. **Egyptian-side support:** up to €150,000, subject to call and award. **Duration:** up to 2 years. Themes include agriculture/sustainable food, water, renewable energy, environment, construction, transport, tourism, pharmacy and mobility. Requires Egyptian PhD-level researcher affiliated with Egyptian research institution, Egyptian industrial partner and Spanish industrial partner. This is R&D / technology transfer, not ordinary platform operations.  
**Wakeel fit:** only genuine applied R&D (e.g. validated industrial demand/supplier matching, traceability or resource-efficiency analytics) with eligible research and industrial partners.  
Official: [CDTI call notice](https://www.cdti.es/en/noticias/abierta-11a-convocatoria-bilateral-espana-egipto-esip-colaboracion-internacional) · [STDF open grants](https://stdf.eg/web/grants/open) · [Fayoum University notice](https://www.fayoum.edu.eg/en/announcements/Details/4809).

### BIOECONOMY GIANTS — Open Call II
**Status:** open; deadline 31 December 2026, 17:00 CET. **Funding:** €280,000 total call; four pilots; up to €70,000/project and €60,000/entity; 100% eligible costs as lump sum. **Duration:** 7 months, March–September 2027. Scope: high-value bio-based products from secondary/underused agricultural, agri-food or forestry biomass, circular value chains and primary-producer participation. Consortium of 2–4 legal entities from EU or Horizon Europe Associated Countries, at least one primary producer, TRL 6–8. Egypt's Horizon association is relevant to country eligibility, but all other conditions apply.  
**Wakeel fit:** specific agricultural/food supplier value-chain pilot, not the general platform; requires actual bio-based product, producer and TRL evidence.  
Official: [BIOECONOMY GIANTS call](https://bioeconomygiants.com/open-call-2/) · [Eligibility summary](https://innovationbridge.info/ibportal/content/bioeconomy-giants-open-call-ii-scaling-and-piloting-high-value-bio-based-products).

### STDF–China Research Fund — Call 9
**Status:** reported open; deadline 15 December 2026 at 14:00 Cairo time. Verify live STDF portal and full call before action. **Published Egyptian-side ceilings:** up to EGP 3m for eligible joint research in water, agriculture, AI, ICT and advanced manufacturing; up to EGP 5m for specified strategic renewable-energy/quantum projects with industrial partner; up to 24 months.  
**Wakeel fit:** genuine Egyptian–Chinese research collaboration only; not general platform or commercial operating finance.  
Official portal: [STDF open grants](https://stdf.eg/web/grants/open). Public notice: [Ahram Gate summary](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/5981770.aspx).

## 4. Applicant architecture and safeguards

| Work package | Appropriate route | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial platform | Eligible for-profit company; develoPPP after proof of concept/matching | Prototype qualifies |
| Marketer training/inclusion | Eligible non-profit or training institution | Grant can be transferred without approved procurement |
| Artisan / women-led producers | Eligible cooperative, non-profit or business under call | Every women-focused call accepts marketplace |
| Nature-based project | Eligible local non-profit under IKI Small Grants | Commercial platform is eligible lead |
| Green skills / coasts | German lead + Egyptian implementing partner under IKI Medium | Egyptian company can lead alone |
| Industrial R&D | Research institution + industrial partners | Ordinary product development automatically eligible |
| Bio-based value chain | Consortium + primary producer + TRL 6–8 | Generic catalogue qualifies |

For every call document applicant, beneficiary, partner roles, country, theme, matching finance, eligible costs, procurement, payment schedule, tax/audit rules, IP/data ownership, conflicts and sustainability. EGP execution contract, 0.5% marketer share, 0.1% platform share and grants are separate streams.
