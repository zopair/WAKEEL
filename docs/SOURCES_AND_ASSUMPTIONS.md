Commercial directories and RFQ sources such as Europages are lead discovery only; verify buyer identity, intent and solvency independently.

## 4. Rules for adding numbers
Every figure needs definition, unit, population, geography, period, publication date, source URL, extraction date, method and caveat. Distinguish calendar/fiscal years and non-oil/total exports. Do not combine unlike denominators or periods without reconciliation. No TAM/SAM/SOM share, five-year revenue, jobs, income, carbon saving or grant receipt without transparent calculation and evidence.
